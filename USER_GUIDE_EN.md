# HabitBet - User Guide

## What is HabitBet?

HabitBet is a habit-building app that uses charitable pledges to keep you accountable. Create a challenge, choose an amount and a charity, and invite a friend as an arbiter. Complete the challenge — nothing happens. Don't complete it — you pledge to donate that amount to charity, and your arbiter verifies the donation.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Sign In

Open the app and tap **"Continue with Google"** (or **"Continue with Apple"** on iOS). Your account is created automatically.

### 2. Home Screen

After signing in you'll see the home screen with two tabs:

| Tab | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| **My Challenges** | Challenges you created |
| **To Arbitrate** | Challenges where you are the arbiter |

Tap the **profile icon** in the top right for your stats and settings.

---

## Creating a Challenge

1. Tap the **"+"** button on the home screen
2. Fill in the challenge details:

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Title** | Short name for your challenge | "Morning Run 5km" |
| **Description** | What exactly counts as completing the task | "Run at least 5km before 9 AM, screenshot from running app as proof" |
| **Duration** | How many days the challenge lasts (7–90) | 21 days |
| **Days per week** | How many days per week you must complete the task | 5 days/week |
| **Pledge amount** | Amount you commit to donate if you don't complete ($5–$500) | $25 |
| **Charity** | Where the donation goes if you don't complete | Rusoriz, Come Back Alive, or Arbiter's Choice |
| **Arbiter** | A friend who will verify your daily proofs | Search by email |

3. Tap **"Create Challenge"**
4. The challenge is created with status **"Waiting for Arbiter"** — you cannot submit proofs yet
5. Your arbiter receives a notification and must accept the invitation
6. Once the arbiter accepts, the challenge becomes **Active** and starts the next day after acceptance

> **Note:** If the arbiter takes several days to respond, the challenge dates adjust automatically. The start date is always the day after the arbiter accepts, so you never lose days while waiting.

## Daily Routine

### Submitting Proof

Every day during your challenge:

1. Go to **My Challenges** and tap your active challenge
2. Find today's date in the day grid
3. Tap on the day → **"Submit Proof"**
4. Take a photo or choose from gallery — this is your evidence
5. Add an optional note explaining what you did
6. Tap **"Submit"**

Your proof is sent to your arbiter for review.

### Rejected Proofs & Resubmission

If your arbiter rejects a proof, you can resubmit — up to **3 attempts per day**:

1. The rejected day will show an orange **refresh icon** with the attempt counter (e.g., "1/3")
2. Tap on the day to see the rejection reason and submit a new proof
3. The bottom sheet will show **"Attempt 2/3"** (or 3/3) so you know how many tries remain
4. If all 3 attempts are rejected, the day turns red and counts as not completed — same as a missed day

> **Tip:** Read the arbiter's rejection reason carefully before resubmitting. Make sure your new proof clearly addresses their feedback.

### Deadlines

- You must submit proof before **23:59** on the day of the task
- If you miss the deadline, the day is automatically marked as **missed**
- You'll receive a reminder notification at **20:00** if you haven't submitted yet

|--------|-------|---------|

## Being an Arbiter

If a friend invites you as an arbiter:

### Accepting an Invitation

1. Go to the **To Arbitrate** tab
2. You'll see pending requests at the top
3. Review the challenge details, then tap **Accept** or **Decline**

### Reviewing Daily Proofs

1. Tap an accepted challenge to open the arbitration screen
2. You'll see submitted proofs waiting for review
3. For each proof you can:
   - **Approve** — the proof is valid, the day counts as completed
   - **Reject** — the proof is insufficient (you must provide a reason)

### Verifying Donations (if the challenge is not completed)

If your friend doesn't complete the challenge:

1. They are asked to donate the pledged amount to the chosen charity
2. They submit a screenshot of the donation receipt through the app
3. You review the receipt and can:
   - **Confirm** — the donation is verified. You also rate the participant's reliability (1–5 stars) and can leave optional feedback
   - **Reject** — the receipt is insufficient (with a reason). They can resubmit

### Arbiter Guidelines

- Be fair and consistent
- Review proofs promptly (your friend is counting on you)
- Only approve proofs that genuinely meet the challenge criteria
- If rejecting, explain why so your friend knows what to improve

## Challenge Outcomes

### Challenge Completed

If you meet the required number of approved days:
- The challenge is marked as **Completed**
- No donation required
- Your profile stats update

### Challenge Not Completed

If you don't meet the required number of approved days:
- The challenge is marked as **Not Completed**
- You are asked to donate the pledged amount to the chosen charity
- Submit a screenshot of your donation receipt through the app
- Your arbiter verifies the donation and rates your reliability

### How Days Are Counted

The app calculates whether you succeeded based on:
- **Duration**: total days of the challenge
- **Required days per week**: how many days per week you committed to
- **Total required days** = (duration / 7) × required days per week (rounded up)
- If your **approved days ≥ total required days**, you succeed

**Example**: 21-day challenge, 5 days/week = need at least 15 approved days.

## Reliability Rating

Your reliability rating reflects how trustworthy you are:

- When a challenge is not completed, your arbiter rates you from **1 to 5 stars** after verifying your donation
- The rating appears on your profile as an average across all reviews
- A high rating shows you honor your commitments — even when you don't complete a challenge
- The more challenges you complete or the more donations you follow through on, the higher your rating

## Notifications

HabitBet sends push notifications for:

- **Daily reminder** (20:00) — if you haven't submitted today's proof
- **Proof submitted** — arbiter is notified when you submit proof
- **Arbiter invitation** — when someone invites you to arbitrate
- **Arbiter response** — when your arbiter accepts or declines
- **Donation proof submitted** — arbiter is notified when you submit a donation receipt

Tapping a notification takes you directly to the relevant challenge.

Make sure notifications are enabled in your phone settings for HabitBet.

## Tips for Success

1. **Start small** — pick a 7-day challenge first to test the flow
2. **Choose a reliable arbiter** — someone who will review proofs daily
3. **Set a daily routine** — do the task and submit proof at the same time each day
4. **Don't ignore reminders** — pay attention to the 20:00 notification
5. **Be specific** — write a clear description so your arbiter knows exactly what to approve
6. **Take clear photos** — make it obvious you completed the task

## Troubleshooting

### "Not logged in" on Profile
Close and reopen the app. Firebase auth session restores automatically on restart.

### Google Sign-In not working
Make sure you have a Google account set up on your device and Google Play Services is up to date.

### Proof photo not uploading
- Check your internet connection
- Make sure the photo is under 5 MB
- Only image files (JPEG, PNG) are supported

### Not receiving notifications
1. Go to phone Settings > Apps > HabitBet > Notifications
2. Make sure notifications are enabled
3. Check that battery optimization is not blocking the app

### Challenge not showing on To Arbitrate tab
The arbiter must sign in with the same email that the challenge creator entered. Check the arbiter email matches exactly.
